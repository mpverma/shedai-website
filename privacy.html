Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 8, 2026  ·  Effective: May 4, 2026

👋 Overview

ShedAI is a product of Vertexa Labs LLC, a California limited liability company ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and share your information when you use the ShedAI mobile application.

Your meal data and personal health information are stored locally on your device and securely in your account. We do not sell your data to third parties. Ever.

🤖 Third-Party AI Service — Anthropic (Claude AI)

This is the most important section of this policy. ShedAI uses Anthropic's Claude AI to power its core features. When you log a meal or request a recommendation, certain data is transmitted to Anthropic's servers for processing.

Who receives your data: Anthropic, PBC

Company: Anthropic, PBC — an AI safety company based in San Francisco, CA.

Service used: Claude AI API (claude.anthropic.com)

Purpose: To analyse your meal descriptions and calculate accurate nutritional information including calories, protein, carbohydrates, and fats.

Anthropic's Privacy Policy: anthropic.com/privacy

Anthropic's Terms of Service: anthropic.com/terms

Exact data sent to Anthropic for each meal log:

Data Type Example Sent to Anthropic?
Meal description text "2 eggs, dal, 2 rotis and chai" YES — sent
Remaining daily calorie target "735 calories remaining" YES — sent
Remaining daily protein target "87g protein remaining" YES — sent
Body metrics for goal calculation Height, weight, goal weight, age (numbers only) YES — sent
Your name — NEVER sent
Your email address — NEVER sent
Your location — NEVER sent
Voice recordings Only transcribed text is sent, not the audio recording NEVER sent
Meal photos Photo of your plate when using Photo meal logging YES — sent
Device identifiers — NEVER sent
All data is transmitted over HTTPS encryption. No personal identifiers (name, email, phone) are ever included in requests to Anthropic's API.

Anthropic may process and retain API request data according to their own privacy policy and data retention practices. We encourage you to review Anthropic's privacy policy at anthropic.com/privacy.

⚠️ User Consent: Before using ShedAI's AI features, users are shown a clear in-app consent screen explaining exactly what data is sent to Anthropic and why. Users must explicitly agree before any data is transmitted to Anthropic's servers.

📱 Information We Collect

Information you provide:

  • Meal descriptions you enter or speak
  • Body metrics (height, weight, goal weight, age)
  • Activity level and dietary preferences
  • Weight log entries
  • Daily calorie and protein goals
  • Email address (for account creation)

Information collected automatically:

  • App usage data (crashes, performance) via Expo
  • Device type and operating system version

💾 Data Storage

Your personal data is stored in two places:

  • Locally on your device — using AsyncStorage for fast, offline access
  • Securely in the cloud — using Supabase (supabase.com) to back up your data so it is not lost if you delete and reinstall the app
Your cloud data is protected by Row Level Security — only you can access your own data. Supabase's privacy policy is available at supabase.com/privacy.

🎤 Microphone Access

ShedAI requests microphone access to enable voice meal logging. The microphone is only activated when you explicitly tap the Log Meal button. Your voice is converted to text locally on your device using Apple's Speech Recognition framework — the audio recording itself is never stored or transmitted anywhere. Only the resulting text description is sent to Anthropic for nutrition analysis.

📸 Camera & Photo Library Access

ShedAI requests camera and photo library access to enable photo meal logging. When you tap Photo to log a meal, you can take a new photo or choose from your library. The photo is sent to Anthropic's Claude AI to identify food items and estimate nutritional content. Photos are transmitted securely over HTTPS and are not stored on our servers.

⚕️ Medical Disclaimer

ShedAI provides estimated nutritional information based on:

  • USDA Dietary Guidelines (dietaryguidelines.gov)
  • Mifflin-St Jeor BMR equation (Journal of the American Dietetic Association, 1990)
  • NIH Health Information (nih.gov/health-information)
ShedAI is not a medical application. The information provided is for general wellness purposes only and is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease. Always consult a qualified healthcare provider before making significant changes to your diet.

👶 Children's Privacy

ShedAI is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.
