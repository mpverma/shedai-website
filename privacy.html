Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 1, 2026  ·  Effective: May 1, 2026

👋 Overview

ShedAI ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use the ShedAI mobile application.

Your meal data and personal health information are stored locally on your device. We do not sell your data to third parties. Ever.

📱 Information We Collect

Information you provide:

  • Meal descriptions you enter or speak
  • Body metrics (height, weight, goal weight, age)
  • Activity level and dietary preferences
  • Weight log entries
  • Daily calorie and protein goals

Information collected automatically:

  • App usage data (crashes, performance) via Expo
  • Device type and operating system version

🤖 Use of AI (Claude by Anthropic)

ShedAI uses Anthropic's Claude AI to power its core features. When you log a meal or request a recommendation, the following data is sent to Anthropic's API:

  • Your meal description text (e.g. "2 eggs and toast")
  • Your remaining daily calorie and protein targets (numbers only)
  • Body metrics for personalised goal calculation (numbers only)
No names, email addresses, or personal identifiers are ever sent to Anthropic's API. All data is transmitted over HTTPS encryption. Anthropic's privacy policy is available at anthropic.com/privacy.

Anthropic may process this data according to their own privacy policy. We encourage you to review it at anthropic.com/privacy.

💾 Data Storage

All your personal data — meal logs, weight entries, and settings — is stored locally on your device using AsyncStorage. This means:

  • Your data does not leave your device (except for AI processing described above)
  • We do not have access to your meal logs or weight data
  • Deleting the app will delete your local data
  • Data is not backed up to our servers

🎤 Microphone Access

ShedAI requests microphone access to enable voice meal logging. The microphone is only activated when you explicitly tap the Log Meal button. Audio is processed locally by your device's speech recognition — we do not record or store your voice.

📸 Photo Library Access

ShedAI may request access to your photo library for future features. We do not currently access, store, or transmit any photos from your device.

⚕️ Medical Disclaimer

ShedAI provides estimated nutritional information based on:

  • USDA Dietary Guidelines (dietaryguidelines.gov)
  • Mifflin-St Jeor BMR equation (Journal of the American Dietetic Association, 1990)
  • NIH Health Information (nih.gov/health-information)
ShedAI is not a medical application. The information provided is for general wellness purposes only and is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease. Always consult a qualified healthcare provider before making significant changes to your diet.

👶 Children's Privacy

ShedAI is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.

🔄 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any significant changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of ShedAI after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

🌍 Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights regarding your personal data including:

  • Right to access your data
  • Right to delete your data (you can delete all data by uninstalling the app)
  • Right to opt out of data processing
  • Right to data portability

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at privacy@getshedai.com.
