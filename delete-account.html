Delete Your Account

Last updated: June 20, 2026

📱 Delete from the app (fastest)

You can permanently delete your ShedAI account and all associated data directly from the app:

  1. Open the ShedAI app
  2. Go to Settings
  3. Tap Delete Account
  4. Confirm the deletion when prompted
In-app deletion is processed immediately and cannot be undone.

✉️ Don't have the app installed?

If you no longer have ShedAI installed, you can request deletion by emailing us from the address associated with your account:

privacy@getshedai.com

Please include the email address you used to sign up, and put "Account Deletion Request" in the subject line. We'll confirm once it's done.

Email requests are processed within 30 days, in line with applicable data protection requirements.

🍽️ Delete individual entries

You don't have to delete your whole account to remove specific data. You can delete individual meal logs or weight entries at any time, directly in the app:

  1. Open the ShedAI app
  2. Go to your meal log or weight history
  3. Find the entry you want to remove
  4. Swipe left on the entry (or tap and hold) and select Delete
Individual entries are removed immediately and cannot be recovered.

🗑️ What gets deleted (full account)

Deleting your account permanently removes the following data:

Data Deleted?
Profile information (name, email) Yes
Meal logs and nutrition history Yes
Weight log entries and goals Yes
Meal photos you've submitted Yes
Locally cached data on your device Yes
⚠️ This action is permanent. Once your account is deleted, this data cannot be recovered.

⏱️ Retention after deletion

Once a deletion request is processed, your data is removed from our active database. Some data may persist briefly in encrypted backups for a limited period before being permanently purged, consistent with our standard backup rotation. We do not use deleted account data for any purpose during this window.
